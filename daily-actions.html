Small Actions Every Day

Simple things you can do at home to reduce air pollution!

My Daily Checklist:

  1. Turn off lights when leaving the room.
  2. Walk or ride a bike for short distances.
  3. Plant a small flower or tree in your balcony.
  4. Do not waste paper or plastic bags.

Every small habit helps clean the air for everyone!
