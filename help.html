How to Stop Air Pollution

We can clean our air if we work together and take real action!

3 Main Solutions:

  • Use Clean Energy: Switch to solar and wind power.
  • Plant Trees: Trees absorb bad gases and produce fresh oxygen.
  • Stop Burning Trash: Recycle plastic and paper instead of burning them.

Fresh air makes our heart and lungs happy and healthy!

Start today and help save the air!
