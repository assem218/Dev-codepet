What is Air Pollution?

Air pollution happens when dangerous gases and dust mix into the air we breathe.

It is super bad for our health, trees, and animals. Dirty air hurts everyone!

Main Sources of Pollution

Air gets dirty mainly from three things:

  • Factory smoke
  • Car exhausts
  • Burning trash
