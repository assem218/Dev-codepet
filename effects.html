Bad Effects of Dirty Air

Air pollution causes big problems for people and planet Earth.

1. Health Problems

Breathing bad air hurts our lungs and causes asthma and coughing.

2. Environmental Damage

Dirty air makes acid rain and harms green trees and wild animals.

Top 3 Most Affected Groups:

  1. Young children
  2. Old people
  3. Animals in forests
